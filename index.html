Questions

What hotels ask before the pilot.

Do I need to replace my reservation system?

No. StayDesk has its own reservations and room board, and it can also run beside the system you already use while your team gets used to it.

What phones does my team need?

A phone with an up-to-date browser. Nothing to install from a store; they open a link and log in with their PIN. Tested on Chrome; during the pilot we check it on your team’s own phones, iPhone included.

What happens if the internet goes down?

The app keeps opening and saving on each device. With the optional hotel server, devices catch up with each other when the connection returns.

Is it in Spanish?

Yes. Each person chooses English or Spanish, and guest quick replies go out in the guest’s language.

How is guest data protected?

Each person sees only what their role allows and logs in with their own PIN. Guests’ ID dates of birth are not stored. StayDesk is in its pilot stage; we review data handling with each hotel before it goes live.

What does the pilot cost?

Nothing for the first month. After that it is $6 per room per month, with no setup fee. If it doesn’t work for you, you stop.
