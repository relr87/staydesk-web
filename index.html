Questions

What hotels ask before the pilot.

Email the founder

Do I need to replace my hotel’s reservation system?

No. StayDesk has its own reservations and room board, and it can also run beside the system you already use.

What phones does my team need?

A phone with an up-to-date browser. Nothing to install from a store: they open a link and log in with their PIN.

What happens if the hotel’s internet goes down?

The app keeps opening and saving on each device, and catches up when the connection returns.

How does the app protect guest data?

Each person sees only what their role allows and logs in with their own PIN. Birth dates from guest IDs are not stored.
